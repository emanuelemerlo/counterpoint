==============================================================================
 CONTRAPPUNTO - plugin for MuseScore 4
 Writes, compares and checks counterpoint
 Version {VERSION} - {DATE}
==============================================================================

Writes a counter-melody in counterpoint for a melody (cantus firmus) and,
if there is one, a bass, on a new staff or in a voice of an existing staff.
It also checks a voice written by hand with the same rules and regenerates a
variant around the notes you want to keep.

Italiano: vedi LEGGIMI.TXT nella stessa cartella.

Step-by-step guides and a short introduction to counterpoint for people who
have never studied it: {WIKI_URL}

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 FEATURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 * Species of counterpoint: free (rhythm complementary to the melody),
   1:1 note against note, 2:1, 4:1, suspensions (4th species), florid
   (5th species).
 * Position: above the melody, inner voice between melody and bass, or as
   the bass (when there is no bass).
 * Strict (academic) or free rules (appoggiaturas, dominant sevenths,
   hidden fifths and octaves tolerated).
 * Correctness: no parallel fifths or octaves; dissonances only as passing
   or neighbour notes, prepared suspensions or dominant sevenths; no voice
   crossing; leading note not doubled.
 * Melodic line: mostly stepwise, leaps recovered by contrary motion, a
   single climax (in the phrase around 60% of the piece), preference for
   contrary motion, cadences approached by step.
 * Instrument range: notes always stay within the range of the destination
   instrument; if needed the voice changes position (above/below the
   melody) or octave, recomputing the line.
 * Altered notes: the counterpoint follows chords outside the key and the
   accidentals of melody and bass (no cross-relations).
 * Automatic detection of key, mode (major/minor) and meter
   (simple/compound), key changes included.
 * Imitation: in the melody's rests the counterpoint echoes its last motif;
   optional imitative entry (free and florid).
 * Third-species figures: cambiata and double neighbour.
 * No outlined tritones or sevenths; two-voice final cadence.
 * Analysis of a written voice: errors in red, warnings in orange.
 * Regenerate the rest: keeps the notes you choose and recomputes the
   others.
 * Settings are remembered from one session to the next.
 * Deterministic result: same settings and same seed always give the same
   counterpoint.
 * Several variants ranked by quality, in the same destination or on
   separate staves.
 * Interface in English and Italian: it follows MuseScore's language, or
   you choose it in the window ("Language"). More languages can be added
   with one file in i18n/.
 * The window follows MuseScore's light/dark theme.
 * Every generation is a single operation, undoable with Ctrl+Z.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 INSTALLATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 1. Unzip: the archive contains the folder "contrappunto" (lower case).
 2. Copy the "contrappunto" folder into MuseScore's plugin folder:
      Windows : Documents\MuseScore4\Plugins\
      macOS   : ~/Documents/MuseScore4/Plugins/
      Linux   : ~/Documents/MuseScore4/Plugins/
    (the folder is also shown in Preferences > Folders > Plugins).
    When updating from an earlier version, first delete the old folder,
    even if it is called "Contrappunto" with a capital C (on Linux both
    folders would stay and the plugin would appear twice), then RESTART
    MuseScore: while it stays open it keeps using the version loaded at
    start-up. The version in use is shown next to the title of the
    plugin's window.
 3. In MuseScore: menu Plugins > Manage plugins, enable "Contrappunto"
    (up to version 2.6 it was called "Contrappunto automatico").
 4. The plugin appears in the menu Plugins > Composing/arranging tools.

The folder must contain:
   contrappunto.qml        interface and connection to the score
   contrappunto_engine.js  musical engine
   i18n/                   interface texts, one file per language
   README.TXT, LEGGIMI.TXT these instructions, in English and Italian


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 HOW TO USE IT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Writing a counterpoint
 1. (Optional) Select a range of bars. Without a selection the plugin
    works on the whole score. It always works on whole bars.
 2. Start the plugin. If you change the selection while the window is
    open, press "Use current selection".
 3. Sources: choose the melody's staff and the bass's staff, or "none" if
    you only have the cantus firmus. Melody and bass can share a staff:
    the highest note is the melody, the lowest the bass.
 4. Destination: "New staff" (with the chosen instrument) or
    "Existing staff" + voice (for example voice 2 of the left hand).
    Voice 1 of a staff that holds the melody or the bass is not allowed.
 5. Style: species, position, register, rules, mode and meter.
 6. Press "Generate".

Trying other solutions
 * After the first generation on a new staff, that staff becomes the
   destination: change the seed ("New seed") and press "Generate" again to
   replace the result with an alternative.
 * "Variety" (0-10) decides how far solutions may stray from the most
   correct one: with 0 every seed gives the same result.

Comparing variants
 1. Set "How many" to a value from 2 to 8 and press "Generate N variants".
 2. The plugin computes the variants (consecutive seeds, shown next to the
    field) and lists them from the best. For each one it shows the score,
    the percentage of stepwise and of contrary motion, the number of
    climaxes, whether there are parallels and the errors and warnings
    found by the analysis (with equal parallels, fewer errors come first).
 3. The best one is already written in the destination. Click another
    variant to replace it and hear it; its seed goes into the "Seed" field,
    so you can find it again later.
 4. Or switch on "One variant per new staff": each variant goes to a new
    staff labelled "Variant N - seed S". Listen, keep the one you like and
    delete the other staves (or press Ctrl+Z).

Analyzing a voice
 1. Under Destination choose "Existing staff" and the voice to check
    (after a generation on a new staff it is already that staff).
 2. Press "Analyze voice". Notes with an error turn red, notes with a
    warning orange; the list with bar numbers appears in the window.
 3. Correct and press "Analyze voice" again: the colours of the previous
    run are removed. Ctrl+Z removes the colours straight away.

Regenerating the rest
 1. In the generated voice, edit by hand the notes you want to keep, or
    select them with Ctrl+click.
 2. Press "Regenerate the rest": those notes stay as they are and all the
    others are recomputed around them, with the same settings.

Changing language
 * Top right, "Language": "Automatic" uses MuseScore's language (English
   if there is no file for that language). The choice is remembered.
   Switching language also translates the message on screen.

Tips
 * For a short cantus firmus try 1:1, 2:1 and suspensions; for a whole
   piece the "Free" species gives the most natural result.
 * Write down the seed of the variants you like: with the same settings
   the result is repeated exactly.
 * If the ties cannot be created, the plugin says so and the notes stay
   repeated instead of tied.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 OPTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 Species / rhythm Free, 1:1, 2:1, 4:1, Suspensions (4th species), Florid (5th)
 Position         Automatic, above the melody, inner voice, as the bass
 Register         Automatic, Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Bass
 Rules            Strict (academic) or Free
 Mode / Meter     Automatic, or forced
 Imitation        In the melody's rests it echoes its last motif
 Imitative entry  (free and florid) Waits while the melody states its
                  opening, then imitates it at the octave, fifth or fourth
 Ties             Ties the held notes
 Variety / Seed   How far from the most correct solution; same seed =
                  same result
 How many         Number of variants to compute and rank (1-8)
 One per staff    Writes each variant on a new staff
 Language         Automatic (MuseScore's), English, Italiano


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 KNOWN LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 * One counterpoint voice at a time.
 * Triplets and other tuplets in the melody are read, but the
   counterpoint uses only binary and dotted values.
 * In 1:1 the climax may be reached two or three times, always in the
   central phrase of the piece.
 * Where melody and accompaniment are very close the counterpoint may rest
   briefly to avoid parallel fifths or octaves.
 * Where the melody leaves the voice's register, the voice crosses to the
   other side of the melody (voice exchange) for those bars.
 * The imitative entry needs at least three notes at the start of the
   melody; with a bass already written it often imitates only part of the
   intervals.
 * "Regenerate the rest" uses the selected notes if your version of
   MuseScore lets plugins read the selection; otherwise the notes edited by
   hand.
